## XDA (English, XenForo BBCode)

```
[B]BitPerfect v5.6 — audio engine for QF / Roco K706 head units (UIS7862)[/B]

A Magisk module. It takes the sound from any player with no attenuation and no change to the frequency response and hands it to the sound processor at [B]24 bit / 48 kHz, 0 dB[/B]. Volume and effects stay in the processor: BU32107 or BD37xxx.

[COLOR=rgb(226, 80, 65)][B]⚠️ UIS7862 only. NOT for UIS8581![/B][/COLOR] On any other platform the installer refuses to install and changes nothing.

[B]Changes since the classic v4.18:[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Hardware:[/B] the sound processor is detected correctly. BU32107 runs over I2S, BD37xxx through the SoC DAC, and the profile is picked automatically. In 4.x every BD unit got the I2S profile, which caused the "digital rasp".
[*][B]Sound path:[/B] one 24/48 output at 0 dB with no digital attenuation. Silent TTS and the "PCM busy" errors are gone.
[*][B]Navigation:[/B] navigator and TTS prompts duck the music, including navigators that announce themselves as an "assistant".
[*][B]SIM calls:[/B] echo cancelling, microphone about +13 dB louder, the bass is back.
[*][B]Bluetooth calls:[/B] louder microphone.
[*][B]Telegram / WhatsApp:[/B] calls work. Before, the other side heard stutter, crackle or silence.
[*][B]Microphone:[/B] a raw input for DSP apps (wDSP) and a clean input for voice assistants. Headroom in every mode; the car-settings microphone sliders turn the level down.
[*][B]Disabling or removing the module[/B] is safe: the factory volume and audio parameters come back right away, with no distortion.
[/LIST]

[B]Install:[/B] flash in Magisk, then reboot. Before installing, the module saves a snapshot of the factory audio state to [ICODE]/sdcard/QF_audio_survey/[/ICODE].

[B]Feedback:[/B] [URL='https://t.me/kostyamat_dev/3']Telegram support group[/URL]
Send the [ICODE]/sdcard/QF_audio_survey/[/ICODE] folder and [ICODE]/data/adb/BitPerfect.log[/ICODE]. For navigation problems or BD37xxx units, please also send a report from the helper module [ICODE]QF_audio_survey.v1.3[/ICODE] (in the repository, [ICODE]tools[/ICODE] folder).

[B]Download:[/B] [URL='https://github.com/kostyamat/BitPerfec-Audio-Qf-module-Magisk/releases/tag/v5.6']BitPerfect v5.6 on GitHub[/URL]
```
